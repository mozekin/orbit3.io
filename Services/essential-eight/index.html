Essential Eight assessments, roadmaps and implementation

The Essential Eight is the Australian Signals Directorate's baseline set of mitigation strategies against cyber threats: patch applications, patch operating systems, multi-factor authentication, restrict administrative privileges, application control, restrict Microsoft Office macros, user application hardening and regular backups. Commonwealth entities are required to implement it, and it increasingly appears in government tenders, enterprise supplier questionnaires and cyber insurance renewals.

Orbit3 tests where you actually sit against each strategy using ASD's maturity model, rather than a self-assessment spreadsheet, then builds a prioritised roadmap and implements the controls with your team. The work overlaps heavily with good cloud security and day-to-day managed IT and cloud operations, so once you reach your target level we can keep you there.

New to the maturity levels? Read Essential Eight maturity levels explained.

  • Maturity assessmentEach of the eight strategies tested against ASD's maturity model, with the evidence recorded.
  • Gap report and roadmapWhat stands between you and your target maturity level, ranked by risk and effort.
  • ImplementationPatching, MFA, privileged access, application control, macro and hardening policies, and backups put in place.
  • Ongoing evidenceControls monitored and re-assessed so your maturity level holds after the project ends.

How we deliver it

Assess, uplift, maintain

ASD's guidance is to bring all eight strategies to the same maturity level before aiming higher. Our roadmap follows that, so effort goes where it lifts your overall level.

Assess

We test each strategy against the maturity model and give you an evidenced maturity level per strategy, plus the gap list. You keep the report whether or not you continue.

Uplift

We implement the controls in priority order with your team, using Microsoft Intune, Defender, Entra ID and the tooling you already pay for wherever possible.

Maintain

Controls are monitored, exceptions reviewed and evidence kept current under managed services, with a re-assessment at least annually.

Platforms

Windows, Microsoft 365 and the cloud

Most Essential Eight controls live in Windows, Microsoft 365 and your identity provider. We implement them with the tools you already license, and extend equivalent controls to your cloud workloads.

Windows and Microsoft 365

Intune for patching and configuration, Defender for Endpoint, Entra ID Conditional Access and Privileged Identity Management, App Control for Business and AppLocker, and Office macro policy. This is where most of the Essential Eight lives.

Microsoft Azure

AWS and Google Cloud

Systems Manager Patch Manager and AWS Backup, or Google Cloud's VM Manager and Backup and DR, with MFA enforced through your identity provider. ASD designed the Essential Eight for Windows networks, so cloud workloads often need equivalent controls rather than a literal match.

Questions we get asked

Frequently asked questions

What is the Essential Eight?

A set of eight mitigation strategies published by the Australian Signals Directorate (ASD) as a baseline against cyber threats: patch applications, patch operating systems, multi-factor authentication, restrict administrative privileges, application control, restrict Microsoft Office macros, user application hardening and regular backups. The accompanying maturity model defines what each strategy looks like at Maturity Levels One to Three.

Which maturity level do we need?

It depends on who is asking. Non-corporate Commonwealth entities must reach Maturity Level Two under the Protective Security Policy Framework, and many government tenders and enterprise customers ask suppliers for the same. For most private businesses, Maturity Level One across all eight strategies is the sensible first target, with Level Two where the threat or the contract warrants it.

Can we get Essential Eight certified?

There is no formal Essential Eight certification. What you can have is an assessment against ASD's maturity model that states your maturity level for each strategy, with the evidence behind it. That is what customers, insurers and boards usually want to see.

Does the Essential Eight apply to cloud and SaaS?

ASD designed the Essential Eight to protect Microsoft Windows-based, internet-connected networks. Cloud services, Linux servers and SaaS platforms still need equivalent protections such as MFA, patching, least privilege and backups, and we assess and implement those alongside the eight strategies rather than forcing a literal fit.

How long does an Essential Eight uplift take?

The assessment takes days to a couple of weeks, depending on size. Reaching Maturity Level One is commonly a matter of weeks; Level Two usually takes months, because application control and privileged access changes need careful rollout. The roadmap gives you a realistic timeline for your environment.

How does the Essential Eight relate to ISO 27001 or SOC 2?

They overlap. The Essential Eight is a prescriptive technical baseline; ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are broader frameworks that expect controls like these to exist. Work done for one counts towards the others, and we map it so you only do it once.
