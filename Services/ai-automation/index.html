Questions we get asked

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI automation agency?

A team that builds automations using AI models alongside traditional workflow tools, so processes that need judgement, such as reading an email or a document, can be automated rather than just the fixed steps. Orbit3 builds those automations and then runs them for you as a managed service.

What kinds of processes can be automated?

Anything high-volume and repetitive with clear rules for what good looks like: email and enquiry triage, invoice and document processing, CRM and helpdesk updates, approvals, onboarding steps, report generation and data entry between systems. Work that needs genuine judgement on every case is a poor first candidate.

What is the difference between an AI agent and normal automation?

Normal automation follows fixed rules: if this field says X, do Y. An AI agent can interpret unstructured input such as an email, a PDF or a conversation, decide which steps to take and call the tools to take them. The most reliable automations combine both: fixed rules for what is predictable and AI for what is not.

Will the automation make mistakes?

Occasionally, as people do. We design for it: confidence thresholds that send uncertain cases to a person, validation against your business rules, approval before anything irreversible, and a log of every decision so mistakes are caught and corrected.

Do you use tools like n8n, Make or Zapier?

Yes, where they fit. Low-code tools are quick and inexpensive for simple flows. For workflows that handle sensitive data, run at volume or need custom logic, we build in your own cloud account so you keep control of the data and the cost.

How quickly will we see a return?

We choose the first workflow on payback, so most clients see hours saved within weeks of go-live. Discovery puts a number on the expected saving before you commit to the build.
